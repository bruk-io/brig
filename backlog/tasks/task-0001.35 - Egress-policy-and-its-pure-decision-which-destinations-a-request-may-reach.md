## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
decision-0009 items 3 and 4, the pure half. A domain EgressPolicy(allow: tuple[Destination, ...]) with Destination(host, port): an exact hostname, *.suffix (names under the suffix, not the suffix), or an exact IPv4/IPv6 literal; the port always required, no CIDR, no port wildcards; refusals for a malformed entry. A pure decision: given the policy and a request (an authority from CONNECT, an absolute URL, or SOCKS5 with a name or a literal) and, for a name, its resolved addresses, allow or refuse with the reason; a resolved private, loopback, link-local, unique-local or metadata address is refused unless an IP-literal grant names it. Ported in spirit, not code, from the old brig's pure filter (old SPEC decisions 133/134).
